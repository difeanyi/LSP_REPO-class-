# Course Orientation - David Ifeanyi

**Course:** LSP – Fall 2026

## Questions

**1. Are late programming assignments accepted?**
No. Per the syllabus, "No late assignments will be accepted." All required work must be submitted on the assigned due date and time.

**2. Where will official course announcements be posted?**
Official announcements are posted on Piazza. Students are expected to monitor Piazza regularly for announcements, resources, and assignment updates.

**3. How will examination dates be announced?**
Examination dates are listed as TBD in the syllabus to allow flexibility based on the pace of the class. The mid-term and final exam dates will be announced in class and posted on Piazza at least two weeks in advance.

**4. Under what circumstances will a makeup examination be considered?**
Makeup examinations are granted only under extraordinary circumstances beyond the student's control. The student must notify the instructor as soon as reasonably possible and provide supporting documentation verifying they were unable to take the exam at the scheduled time (a diagnosis or private medical details are not required). The instructor evaluates the documentation and circumstances to decide whether a makeup exam will be permitted; a doctor's note or urgent care receipt alone does not automatically qualify.

**5. Is AI permitted in this course?**
Generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Claude, Gemini) may be used only when permitted by the instructor or the specific assignment. Regardless of AI use, students remain fully responsible for the accuracy, originality, testing, documentation, and understanding of all submitted work, and must be able to explain, modify, debug, and defend any code, design, or written material they submit.

**6. Which course policy will have the greatest impact on your success this semester? Explain why (2–3 sentences).**
The strict no-late-assignments policy will have the greatest impact on my success this semester. Because there is no grace period for missed deadlines, I need to plan my time carefully around other coursework and start assignments early rather than relying on the ability to submit late. This makes consistent time management essential to staying on track throughout the semester.

**7. When are office hours for LSP Fall 2026?**
Office hours are provided on an as-needed basis rather than on a fixed weekly schedule; students should coordinate with the instructor directly to arrange a time.

**8. What is your responsibility for monitoring Piazza, and are you responsible for assignment updates, clarifications, or deadline changes posted there?**
Students are expected to monitor Piazza regularly for announcements, resources, and assignment updates. Yes — students are responsible for any assignment updates, clarifications, or deadline changes posted on Piazza; failing to check Piazza is not an acceptable excuse for missing this information.

**9. What documentation is required if you are unable to take an examination because of a medical issue or other approved circumstance?**
The student must provide appropriate supporting documentation that verifies they were unable to participate in the exam at the scheduled time. The documentation does not need to disclose a diagnosis or other private medical information, but a doctor's note, urgent care receipt, or verification of a medical visit does not automatically guarantee a makeup exam — the instructor evaluates the documentation and surrounding circumstances before making a decision.

**10. Does scholarship status entitle a student to different grading, deadline, attendance, or course requirements?**
No. The syllabus does not provide any exception based on scholarship status — all students are held to the same grading criteria, deadlines, attendance expectations, and course requirements.
